# Community Code of Conduct

Genesis HQ welcomes technical disagreement and adversarial review.

The project is stronger when contributors can challenge assumptions, reproduce failures and reject weak evidence without personal friction.

## Expected behavior

- Critique ideas, code and evidence — not people.
- Be specific when claiming a result is wrong.
- Prefer reproducible examples over rhetoric.
- Distinguish measured facts from hypotheses and opinions.
- Respect security and privacy boundaries.
- Do not post credentials, private keys, tokens or personal data.
- Give credit for prior work and sources.
- Accept that `NO_GO`, `REJECTED` and `INSUFFICIENT_DATA` are legitimate outcomes.

## Unacceptable behavior

- harassment, threats or discriminatory abuse;
- intentionally misleading performance claims;
- falsifying or selectively deleting research evidence;
- publishing another person's secrets or private information;
- pressuring contributors to weaken safety gates for better-looking results;
- presenting synthetic/test data as real market evidence.

## Technical disagreements

For contentious research conclusions, prefer this escalation path:

1. define the disputed claim;
2. identify the exact evidence;
3. add or improve a deterministic test/replay;
4. document competing interpretations;
5. let reproducible evidence decide what can be claimed.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions that violate this code and may restrict participation for repeated or severe violations.
