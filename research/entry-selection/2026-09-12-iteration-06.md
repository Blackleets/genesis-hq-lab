## Hypothesis H06 — distance-to-Donchian/SMA conditioned abstention

**Hypothesis:** the magnitude of the breakout beyond the Donchian boundary and/or distance from SMA55 at signal time may identify overextended or marginal entries where abstention improves net expectancy.

This is selected before volume/order-book/funding because Donchian and SMA55 are already part of the entry signal itself and therefore have direct causal relevance to entry quality.

## Leakage / evidence-integrity audit

The current runner computes the entry signal from closed candles only (`rows.slice(0, -1)`). At signal time it has `last`, the prior Donchian `high`/`low`, and `sma55`, so exact signed distances can be computed causally before entry, e.g. `last/high - 1`, `last/low - 1`, and `last/sma55 - 1`.

However, the signal currently returns only `{ go, side, reason }`. It does not return or persist the Donchian boundary, breakout-distance percentage, SMA55, or exact entry-to-SMA distance. The persisted evidence snapshot selects only strategy/version, PnL, timestamps, regime/session and runner metadata. Therefore realized trades cannot be cleanly attributed to raw pre-entry Donchian/SMA distance from repository evidence.

`entry_regime` cannot be used as a proxy for SMA distance here: H02 already evaluated regime-conditioned abstention, and using regime labels to infer a new SMA threshold would duplicate that hypothesis and invite post-selection bias.
