## Hypothesis H05 — volatility-conditioned abstention

**Hypothesis:** pre-entry volatility (`volRatio` / ATR%) may identify entry conditions where abstention improves net expectancy.

## Leakage / evidence-integrity audit

The runner computes `classifyMarketContext(rows)` before `signal(...)` and before `openPaperPosition(...)`. `classifyMarketContext` excludes the still-forming candle (`rows.slice(0, -1)`) and computes `volRatio`, `momentum20`, `smaSpreadPct`, regime and session from closed candles. `atrPct(rows)` likewise excludes the still-forming candle. Therefore these volatility features are causally available pre-entry and can be used for future SHADOW evaluation without look-ahead.

However, `openPaperPosition(...)` currently persists only `entry_regime` and `entry_session` from that context. It does not persist raw `entry_vol_ratio` or `entry_atr_pct`. The cycle decision log also records regime/session for skips but not raw volatility. Consequently there is no clean exact-version PAPER cohort with raw pre-entry volatility values that can be joined to realized outcomes without reconstructing missing features after the fact.
