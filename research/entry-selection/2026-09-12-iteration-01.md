## Hypothesis H01 — NO_TRADE until evidence integrity is sufficient

**Hypothesis:** The highest-value entry-selection improvement currently available is abstention: do not promote any additional entry rule until the candidate strategy has enough correctly attributed forward/shadow observations to estimate net expectancy and Profit Factor without mixing legacy strategy versions.

### Why this hypothesis is first

The canonical economic audit reports:

- exact `short_micro:v8`: 2 closed forward-paper trades, stored PnL -1.530 USD, expectancy -0.765 USD/trade, PF 0.361702;
- latest research snapshot: OOS 9 trades, PnL -4.835 USD, PF 0.5726;
- walk-forward: only 1/3 positive folds where 2 were required;
- 64 historical futures closures are mixed historical attribution and therefore are not a valid sample for tuning the current strategy;
- confidence is constant 0.75 and is not calibrated.

These facts make regime/session/momentum/volatility/volume threshold selection underpowered at this stage. Selecting a threshold from this sample would be optimization on noise.

### Development / validation / holdout

Not split. `n=2` forward PAPER and `n=9` research OOS are too small to create statistically meaningful development, validation and holdout partitions. Creating those partitions now would manufacture precision.

## Decision

**NO_GO** for any new entry threshold/filter in this iteration.

**GO** for the abstention gate as a research policy: collect clean, exact-version PAPER/SHADOW entry features and outcomes before testing a threshold. Historical mixed-version rows may be used only for hypothesis generation, never for promotion evidence.

Minimum promotion requirements for a later entry hypothesis:

1. exact strategy/version attribution;
2. feature values captured at entry (not reconstructed with future data);
3. net outcome with existing cost semantics preserved;
4. enough observations to maintain a genuine untouched holdout; target >=30 exact-version closed observations before treating confidence as more than exploratory, and substantially more for segmented rules;
5. positive net expectancy and PF > 1 on validation **and** holdout, with no material drawdown deterioration versus the unchanged baseline;
6. no parameter change to v9/ratchet/TP/SL/sizing during entry-filter evaluation.

## Next non-duplicative hypothesis

Once sufficient clean observations exist, prioritize **regime-conditioned abstention**: test whether suppressing entries in the weakest pre-entry regime improves net expectancy versus the unchanged baseline. Do not choose the regime cutoff from holdout data.
