## Hypothesis H08 — entry order-book imbalance conditioned abstention

**Hypothesis:** for v9 breakout entries, abstain when contemporaneous order-book imbalance is materially opposed to the intended side. A pre-entry depth signal could reject breakouts facing near-book pressure and improve net expectancy.

This is the next highest-value untested hypothesis because order-book imbalance already exists in the runner and therefore its exact semantics can be audited without inventing a new indicator.

## Leakage / evidence-integrity audit

The runner implements `fetchDepth(pair)` against Binance public spot depth and `orderBookImbalance(depth)`, computing top-level bid/ask USD imbalance. However, the only current call to `fetchDepth()` occurs inside `closePositions()` for an already-open v9 short. That value is passed to `exitContext()` and stored in ratchet/exit context after entry.

The entry path is different: `tick()` obtains klines, classifies market context, computes the Donchian signal, obtains price, and calls `openPaperPosition(...)`. It does **not** fetch depth before opening. `openPaperPosition()` persists entry regime/session and volume proxy, but no entry order-book imbalance or depth timestamp/source.

Therefore the existing imbalance observations are post-entry exit-management observations. Reusing them as an entry feature would introduce temporal leakage. Historical reconstruction of depth after seeing outcomes is not allowed and would not reproduce the exact book Genesis saw at signal time.

## Reproducible evaluation
